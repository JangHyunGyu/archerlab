What is ArcherLab? A hub of browser-based web services by one developer

ArcherLab (archerlab.dev) is a hub of 20 side projects built by developer Hyungyu Jang, combining frontend, backend and AI. It includes a free browser game portal, visual novels, AI fortune and video analysis tools, a news summary, IT history timelines and live chat, all usable in the browser without installing an app. Use the list below to jump to each service guide.

How to use it

  1. Pick a service from the list below and open its guide.
  2. On the guide, check what the service does, its price, whether signup is needed, and the FAQ.
  3. Open the service from the button on the guide or from the link next to its name.

Features

  • A free browser game portal with 10 games
  • Visual novels: school romance and school mystery
  • AI saju and fortune, golf swing analysis and Latin dance video analysis
  • Hacker News summaries in Korean and IT history timelines
  • Live chat with public and private rooms

Good to know

Harem Mate is for adults aged 19 and over only. Mate does not replace professional counseling, and the fortune service is for entertainment.

Frequently asked questions

What is ArcherLab?

A hub of browser-based side projects built by developer Hyungyu Jang, run as a place to build prototypes and record experiments.

Are the services free?

Every service listed here can be started for free, and most need no signup or install. See each service guide for details.

Who makes them?

Hyungyu Jang handles planning, development and operation. Some projects, such as Cupid, have co-creators.

How do I get in touch?

Email hyungyu@archerlab.dev.

Where should I start?

Archerlab Games for a casual start, Cupid or Nevergrad for story games, and Karma for fortune readings.

Are there English guides?

Yes, for every service except HN Top 10 and Chat. HN Top 10 has a Korean guide only, and Chat opens directly without a separate guide.
